Privacy Policy

App: mBlue (com.khacmuoivu.mblue). Last updated: 2026-10-30.

Who we are

mBlue is published by Khac Muoi VU. For privacy questions, email dreamingscientist@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

mBlue keeps app content, settings, and progress on your device. We do not operate a cloud synchronization service for this data. Local data is normally removed when you uninstall the app, subject to backups managed by your device or store account.

Advertising

mBlue uses Google AdMob and may show banner or rewarded video ads. Google may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostics to provide, measure, limit, and protect advertising.

On iOS, mBlue requests App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission before ads. If you deny, restrict, or have not granted ATT, the app does not access IDFA for tracking; Google Mobile Ads may still return eligible non-personalized or limited ads, subject to Google's consent signals and applicable regional choices. Non-personalized ads can still use identifiers for frequency capping and aggregated reporting. ATT permission does not replace any additional consent required for advertising in your region. Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for consent before ads are requested. You can change eligible privacy choices in the app's Settings or manage tracking permission in device settings.

Learn more from Google's partner-sites information, Google's ATT and IDFA guidance, and Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics

mBlue uses Firebase Analytics on iOS and Android to measure basic app usage, including active users, sessions, app opens, and the weather tabs viewed. Analytics uses an app-instance identifier. Advertising storage, advertising user-data use, and ad personalization are disabled for Firebase Analytics. On iOS, the app build excludes Firebase advertising ID support, and vendor identifier collection is disabled. Analytics events do not include city names, coordinates, dates of birth, or account identifiers. Basic analytics remains enabled if you deny ATT.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and message. We use them only to respond, diagnose the reported issue, and improve support.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information associated with support messages or an account. If you are in the European Economic Area, you may also contact your local data protection authority.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The date above identifies the current version.
